## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot exposes a slash command, `/ask <question>`, that forwards the
question you type to a research Agent and streams the Agent's answer back
into the Discord channel where you invoked the command. Long answers may be
delivered as a Discord embed or as an attached Markdown file.

The Bot does not store conversation history: every `/ask` starts a fresh
session.
